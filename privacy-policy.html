Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. About iVenue

iVenue is an educational and demonstration web-development project designed to simulate an online event discovery, ticket reservation and account-management platform.

iVenue does not currently process real ticket payments or issue tickets with monetary value.

Although the platform is educational, registered users may provide real personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information iVenue processes, why it is processed and how users can exercise their rights.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use iVenue, we may process the following information.

Account and Profile Information

This may include:

  • First and last name;
  • Email address;
  • Phone number, where provided;
  • Profile image, where voluntarily uploaded;
  • Account identifier;
  • Account creation and verification information.

Authentication credentials are handled through the platform's authentication infrastructure. Passwords are not displayed to iVenue administrators.

Authentication Information

Users may register or sign in through supported authentication methods, including email authentication and Google authentication.

When Google authentication is used, iVenue may receive information made available through that authentication process, such as the user's email address, name, profile image and provider-specific account identifier.

Demo Reservation and Ticketing Information

iVenue may process information concerning events viewed; tickets or seats selected; cart additions; demo reservations; reservation expiration; and demo orders and associated event information. These functions are provided for educational and demonstration purposes. iVenue does not currently process real payments or issue tickets with monetary value.

Newsletter Information

If a user voluntarily subscribes to the iVenue newsletter, we may process their email address, subscription status and information necessary to manage subscription and unsubscription. Newsletter subscription is optional and is separate from the creation of an iVenue account.

Support Communications

If a user contacts iVenue support, we may process email address, message content, relevant timestamps, support conversation history, and information reasonably necessary to investigate and answer the request.

Security and Policy Information

Where necessary for protecting the platform and its users, iVenue may maintain limited records concerning confirmed violations of the Terms of Use, warnings, temporary restrictions, account suspensions, and administrative actions and the reasons recorded for them.

3. Activity Tracking

iVenue does not currently perform continuous individual activity-time tracking. In particular, iVenue does not calculate how many hours an individual user spends browsing the website and does not intentionally collect detailed mouse movements or similar behavioural-monitoring information.

Normal actions necessary for platform functionality, such as event views, cart additions, reservations and demo orders, may still be recorded where necessary to provide, secure or evaluate the relevant functionality.

4. Why We Process Information

Information may be processed where reasonably necessary to create and maintain user accounts; authenticate users; provide profile functionality; provide demo event, cart, reservation and ticketing functionality; provide customer support; protect accounts and the platform from abuse or unauthorized access; enforce the Terms of Use; maintain security and reliability; manage voluntary newsletter subscriptions; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Personal information should not be used for unrelated purposes merely because it is technically available.

5. Data Minimisation

iVenue seeks to process only information reasonably necessary for a defined purpose. The platform does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through ordinary account functionality. Users should avoid sending sensitive personal information through support messages unless it is genuinely necessary for resolving the request.

6. Newsletter and Marketing Communications

Newsletter subscription is optional. Creating an iVenue account does not automatically subscribe a user to marketing communications. Where consent is required, newsletter consent is requested separately from acceptance of the Terms of Use. Users may unsubscribe through the unsubscribe functionality provided by iVenue. Withdrawing newsletter consent does not affect the user's account or prevent necessary service-related communications.

7. Browser Storage

iVenue may use LocalStorage, cookies or similar browser technologies where necessary for authentication/session functionality, cart and reservation state, interface preferences, and security and technical operation. If non-essential advertising, analytics or behavioural-tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and any necessary consent mechanisms should be updated before they are enabled.

8. Third-Party Services

iVenue uses third-party technology services for database infrastructure, authentication, website hosting, email delivery, and Google authentication. These providers may process information to the extent necessary to provide their respective services.

9. Data Security

iVenue uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These measures may include authentication controls, row-level database access controls, administrative permissions, restricted privileged operations, HTTPS, access controls, and appropriate logging of sensitive administrative actions.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own account access.

10. Administrative Access

Authorized administrators or support personnel may access user information only where reasonably necessary for account administration, support, security, investigation of confirmed or suspected policy violations, technical maintenance, or compliance with applicable obligations. Administrative access must not be used to inspect personal information without a legitimate reason.

11. Account Restrictions

iVenue may maintain records of confirmed violations of the Terms of Use where necessary to protect the platform. Administrative indicators may be used to identify accounts requiring review. Such indicators should not automatically result in permanent suspension solely because a numerical threshold has been reached. Material account restrictions should, where reasonably possible, involve review by an authorized administrator.

12. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed or where continued retention is otherwise required or permitted.

When an account is deleted, account and profile information may be deleted; active cart information may be deleted; certain historical records may be anonymised or disassociated from the account; and security or administrative records may be retained where there is a legitimate and lawful reason. Information should not be retained indefinitely without a defined purpose.

13. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including the ability to request information about its processing, access personal information relating to them, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion where applicable, request restriction or cessation of certain processing where applicable, withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, object to certain processing where provided by law, and exercise other rights available under applicable data-protection legislation.

iVenue may reasonably verify the identity of a requester before fulfilling a request involving personal information.

14. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their iVenue account through available account functionality or an available support channel. Account deletion may permanently remove access to the account. Certain records may be anonymised or retained where there is an appropriate legal, security or technical reason for doing so.

15. Children

iVenue is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children where parental or legal-representative authorization would be required by applicable law. If functionality specifically intended for children is introduced, appropriate safeguards should be implemented first.

16. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when iVenue's functionality, data-processing practices or applicable requirements change. The current version and last-updated date will be displayed on the website. Where a material change requires renewed consent, users should be asked to provide that consent before the new processing is relied upon.

17. Contact

Questions or requests concerning privacy may be submitted through the contact or support functionality available on iVenue. iVenue users may also exercise any rights available to them under applicable Georgian data-protection law.
